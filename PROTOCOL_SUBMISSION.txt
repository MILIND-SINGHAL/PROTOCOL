2. HACKATHON (SHIP-A-THON) SUBMISSION KIT

[PROJECT TITLE]
Protocol - Executive Circadian Wellness & Focus OS

[TAGLINE] (Under 60 chars)
Synchronize your day with science-backed circadian protocols.

[INSPIRATION]
In today’s hyper-connected, high-stress knowledge economy, executives and
creators struggle with irregular sleep cycles, erratic caffeine crashes,
and chronic cognitive fatigue. While many apps track sleep passively, they
rarely provide an actionable, real-time operating system that guides your
biology from the moment you wake up to the moment you power down. We built
Protocol to transform circadian neurobiology (morning sunlight anchoring,
caffeine cut-off countdowns, physiological sigh reset, and evening melatonin
preservation) into a sleek, gamified executive daily routine.

[WHAT IT DOES]
Protocol anchors your daily schedule to your personal wake-up baseline and
dynamically adjusts 4 core daily tracks:
1. Track 1: Morning Sunlight & Hydration (Cortisol awakening reset)
2. Track 2: Peak Cognitive Deep Work (Dopamine and focus synchronization)
3. Track 3: Afternoon Recalibration & Caffeine Cut-Off (Adenosine management)
4. Track 4: Evening Melatonin Defense & Wind-Down (Blue light & nervous system)

Additional features include:
- Interactive Breath Pacer: Guided Box Breathing (4-4-4-4) and Physiological
  Sigh (double inhale, long exhale) with real-time audio and haptic feedback.
- Binaural Focus Soundscapes: Pink noise, Theta waves, and ambient focus loops.
- Habit Streaks & Daily Adherence: Local Room database tracking lifetime
  streaks and daily protocol completion.
- Push Reminders: Timed notifications for morning sunlight and evening
  screen shut-off.

[HOW WE BUILT IT]
- Framework: 100% Modern Kotlin & Jetpack Compose.
- UI Design: Material Design 3 with custom dark/light adaptive color palettes,
  smooth spring animations, and high-contrast typographic hierarchy.
- State & Architecture: MVVM architecture with Kotlin Coroutines, StateFlow,
  and reactive State management.
- Data Persistence: Jetpack Room SQLite database with full offline-first
  capabilities.
- Monetization: RevenueCat Android SDK (v8.12.3) managing subscriptions,
  paywalls, and remote entitlement synchronization.
- Notifications: Protocol notification system with AlarmManager for circadian event scheduling.
- Integrity: Custom SecurityIntegrityManager with zero-client admin attack
  surface and tamper-detection safeguards.

[REVENUECAT MONETIZATION & PAYWALL INTEGRATION]
RevenueCat powers the entire commercial layer of Protocol:
- Gated Entitlement: `pro` entitlement unlocks Tracks 2-4, custom circadian
  wake-up offsets, unlimited breathwork soundscapes, and advanced analytics.
- Dual-Offering Architecture:
  * Annual VIP Protocol: $39.99/year with a 7-day free trial ($3.33/month).
  * Weekly Accelerate Pass: $6.99/week for rapid sprints.
- Remote Experimentation / A/B Testing: Built-in support for remote RevenueCat
  Offerings (`standard` vs `shipaton_experiment_offer`), toggleable directly
  in Settings > Advanced Billing Diagnostics.
- Resilient Fallback Engine: If network or Google Play Billing is temporarily
  unavailable, the app uses an internal cached catalog and local sandbox,
  preventing paywall crashes or blocking user experience.

[INSTRUCTIONS FOR AUDITORS & TESTERS (GOOGLE PLAY LICENSE TESTING)]
Subscription testing is verified through Google Play Billing & RevenueCat:
- Google Play License Testers: Add tester email in Google Play Console & RevenueCat
  Dashboard to test test-purchases without credit card charges.
- Debug Builds: Demo mode can only be enabled in dedicated debug builds via
  BuildConfig.DEMO_MODE, and is strictly disabled and excluded from release.
- Entitlement Authority: Entitlements are verified directly against RevenueCat
  customerInfo.entitlements["pro"].isActive, ensuring genuine authorization.

================================================================================

  guarantee complete resistance against APK patching and smali tampering.
